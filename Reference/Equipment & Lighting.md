# Equipment & Lighting

What we use, and the options worth knowing if the lab grows. The brand lists are jumping-off points, not requirements.

---

## Capture systems

Dedicated pages for the two systems we capture on, with the materials each is used for:

- **[[DT Versa]]** — motorized copy stand with a glass platen; flat material pressed to glass (documents, loose/unbound pages, flat art).
- **[[DT V-Cradle]]** — book cradle for bound volumes captured intact, without stressing the binding.

> [!tip] Which one?
> Press-flat material → **[[DT Versa]]**. Bound volume that must stay intact → **[[DT V-Cradle]]**.

- **Camera:** DT / Phase One iXG / iXH (100–150 MP) — FADGI 4-star class, common in fine-art and CH labs.
- **Column / surface:** DT Auto Column + Atom surface (enables Auto-PPI focusing — see [[PPI & Focus]]).

- **Software:** Capture One **Cultural Heritage (CH)** — adds Base Characteristics Mode and pre-built CH ICC profiles ([[Mode, Profile & Curve]]).
- **Control:** foot pedals raise/lower the platen and trigger capture; a Stream Deck speeds up cropping scripts ([[Cropping & Straightening]]).

## Lighting principles

- Want adjustability of **brightness, color temperature, and position**.
- Prefer **low heat** (LEDs over strobes).
- **Hoods / barn doors** control light direction.
- **Diffusers** matter for shiny/reflective materials — but avoid over-diffusing, which softens detail.
- Brands in CH labs: Digital Transitions (DT Photon XL), Profoto, Broncolor.

## Backgrounds

> Use black backgrounds unless the item is black.

- **Black velvet/velveteen** (or flocked paper) — the pile absorbs light and reads darker than flat black fabric. Keep a lint roller handy.
- Avoid crushed/stretch velvet and very cheap wide-knit velvet (low density). Don't iron it — steam from the back if you must; roll or hang rather than fold.
- **Gray** options: Canson Colorline Pebble Gray, Savage #9 Stone Gray, Savage #60 Focus Gray.
- **White** helps with low-contrast or translucent originals.

## Other capture options (reference)

- **Flatbed scanners:** Epson Expression 10000XL / 13000XL (most common); Plustek OpticBook (book-edge, reduces spine shadow).
- **Document feeders:** Ricoh/Fujitsu fi series, Canon imageFORMULA.
- **Film:** prefer a camera + light box over a dedicated film scanner; Negative Supply and Kaiser make budget film accessories.
- **Large format:** i2S (FADGI 4-star, large footprint).
- **Cameras:** Phase One Heritage (iXH); Sony a7R IV/V (60+ MP) as a lower-cost alternative.

## Calibration gear

- Monitor: X-Rite i1 ([[Color Management & Calibration#The Monitor]]).
- Targets: GoldenThread FADGI 19264, ISA device-level, X-Rite ColorChecker, IT8 (flatbed).

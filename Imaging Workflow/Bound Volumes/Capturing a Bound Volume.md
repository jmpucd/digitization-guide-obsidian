# Capturing a Bound Volume

The bound-volume **imaging technique** for the [[Capture]] phase. Create the session and run preflight as in [[Capture]] / [[General Preflight]] first; this page covers how to physically image the volume.

> [!info] Settings
> 400 ppi · Base Characteristics: Mode **Photography**, ICC **Flat Art Reproduction – LED DT Photon**, Curve **Linear Response** ([[Mode, Profile & Curve]]).
> ![[capture-base-characteristics-bound.png]]

---

## Image the volume

1. **Unbind** the volume; remove the **back cover** and set aside.
2. Place the whole volume on the **right** of the platen.
3. Capture the **front cover** on top of all the pages.
4. Turn the cover to the **left** of the platen; capture the **first page + inside of the cover**.
5. Remove the **front cover** and set aside.
6. Continue imaging as a normal bound volume, moving each imaged page to the **left** pile after each capture.
   - Keep the piles neat — it makes cropping and straightening easier.
7. At the **last page**, flip it to the left as usual, then place the **back cover** on the right platen and image it.
8. Flip the back cover to its **back side** on the left platen and image that.

✅ Imaging complete → **[[Page Splitting]]**.

> [!tip] Keeping a volume intact
> If the volume can't be unbound, capture it in the **[[DT V-Cradle]]** instead — you'll image spreads in the cradle rather than pressed single pages, but the rest of the workflow ([[Page Splitting]] → [[Cropping & Straightening]] → [[Export]] → [[Quality Control]]) is the same.

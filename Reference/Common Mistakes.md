# Common Mistakes

A checklist of what goes wrong, so you can catch it at capture instead of in QC. Pair this with [[Quality Control]].

## By condition

- **Fragile / flaking items** — don't unfold without supervisor sign-off; don't weight ink or painted surfaces.
- **Folded corners** — flatten gently so no information is hidden under a fold.
- **Glossy / reflective items** — watch for glare and silver "mirroring"; adjust light angle or add diffusion ([[Equipment & Lighting]]).
- **Halftone / printed pictures** — risk of **moiré**. Pivot the item ~45°, shoot at higher resolution, or change equipment to break the pattern.
- **Onion-skin / translucent paper** — back with a neutral sheet so the reverse side doesn't show through.
- **Text close to the gutter** — make sure the gutter text isn't cut off (especially bound volumes — [[Bound Volumes]]).
- **Thick / tightly bound items** — use cradles/wedges; don't force the spine flat.

## By material / process

- Pages **not flush** with the glass (out-of-plane blur).
- **Cutting off gutter text** when cropping.
- **Page slope** — keep the page straight to the guide edge (we use the **top edge** — see [[Cropping & Straightening]]).
- **Creased pages and highlights** — creases catch light; reposition or re-light.
- **Film flipped the wrong way** (emulsion side) — see [[Imaging Standards by Material Type#Film Negatives]].
- **Dirty film / dust** — clean and re-check at 100%.
- **Glare on glossy photographs** or on photo creases.
- **Documents captured as photographs** (or vice versa) — wrong resolution/bit depth; confirm the material type first.
- **Wrong resolution for postcards** — postcards are **always 600 ppi**, even drawings.
- **Incorrect orientation** for OCR or for handwritten material.

> [!tip] Catch it early
> Keep the **Focus** tool open at 100% while you work, and glance at the whole frame after each capture. Most of these are obvious before you ever reach QC.

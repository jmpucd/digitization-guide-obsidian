# DT V-Cradle

A Digital Transitions **book cradle** that supports a bound volume in a **V** so it can be captured **without stressing the binding** or forcing the book flat. Used with an overhead camera for bound material that must stay intact.

## What it's for

Bound material you should **not** unbind or press flat:

- **Bound Items: Archival** captured as-found → [[Imaging Standards by Material Type#Bound Items Archival]]
- **Scrapbooks** (fragile, items affixed to pages) → [[Imaging Standards by Material Type#Scrapbooks]]
- Tightly bound, fragile, or valuable volumes — see the cautions in [[Common Mistakes]] (gutter text, tight bindings).

> [!tip] Cradle vs. platen
> Use the **V-Cradle** to keep a volume intact and protect the spine. Use the **[[DT Versa]]** platen when pages are loose/unbound and can be pressed against the glass. The Bodega Bay [[Bound Volumes]] workflow unbinds and uses the Versa platen; the V-Cradle is the alternative for volumes that can't be unbound.

## Handling

- Support the spine; never force the book past its comfortable opening angle.
- Adjust the cradle so each page sits in the focal plane (refocus per [[PPI & Focus]] if the page depth shifts as you turn).
- General rules: [[Handling Archival Materials]].

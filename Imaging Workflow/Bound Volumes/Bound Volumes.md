# Bound Volumes

The [[Imaging Workflow]] applied to **bound objects** — books, periodicals, reports — on the Versa station. This page lists the standard phases plus the two steps unique to bound volumes (**page splitting** and **deleting blank verso pages**).

> [!example] Worked example: the **Bodega Bay** project
> The screenshots and specifics come from the Bodega Bay project, but the workflow applies to any bound volume. Substitute your own session name.

> [!info] Standard for this material
> 400 ppi · 24-bit · Adobe RGB (1998) · TIFF — see [[Imaging Standards by Material Type#Bound Items Archival]].

> [!tip] Equipment
> This workflow **unbinds** the volume and images pages on the **[[DT Versa]]** platen. For volumes that must stay **intact**, use the **[[DT V-Cradle]]** instead.

---

## Steps in order

1. **[[General Preflight]]** — for bound volumes: Mode **Photography**, ICC **Flat Art Reproduction – LED DT Photon**, Curve **Linear Response**.
2. **[[Capture]]** → technique: **[[Capturing a Bound Volume]]** (unbind, image cover-to-cover).
3. **[[Page Splitting]]** ← *bound-specific* — duplicate variants, split spreads, loose auto-crop each side.
4. **[[Cropping & Straightening]]** — precise crop + first-round QC. Bound guideline: straighten to the **top edge**, crop just inside the page.
5. **[[Delete Verso]]** ← *bound-specific* — remove blank back pages.
6. **[[Export]]** — TIFF masters, two-recipe pattern.
7. **[[Quality Control]]** — final QC in Photo Mechanic.
8. Hand off: [[Processing Pipeline Overview]] → [[Session Handoff (digi)]] → [[Archiving & Delivery (archive-tool)]].

Track progress with the [[Finder Color Tag Guide]].

## Handling

- Unbind the volume; remove and set aside the back cover, then the front cover, capturing each.
- Keep imaged pages in a neat pile to the side — neat piles make cropping/straightening easier.
- General rules: [[Handling Archival Materials]]. Watch for gutter text and tight spines ([[Common Mistakes]]).

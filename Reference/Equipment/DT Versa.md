# DT Versa

Our main **reprographic capture station** — a Digital Transitions motorized copy-stand system with a **glass platen** raised to the camera by **foot pedals**, paired with the Phase One iXG/iXH camera and DT Stellar/Photon lights. It presses flat material against glass for even, in-focus, distortion-free capture.

## What it's for

Flat reproduction where the material can be laid against the glass:

- **[[Bound Volumes]]** — unbound pages pressed under the glass (our Bodega Bay workflow runs on the Versa).
- **Documents** → [[Imaging Standards by Material Type#Documents]]
- **Oversized / flat art** within the capture area → [[Large & Flat Art]], [[Imaging Standards by Material Type#Oversized Items]]
- **Photographs / postcards** → [[Imaging Standards by Material Type#Photographs]]

> [!tip] Versa vs. cradle
> Use the **Versa platen** when the item can be pressed flat (loose pages, documents, unbound volumes). Use the **[[DT V-Cradle]]** when a bound volume must stay intact or shouldn't be pressed flat.

## How we use it

- **Foot pedals** raise/lower the platen and trigger capture; a light-control box switches the two lights. See the hardware steps in [[General Preflight#1. Room & hardware setup]].
- Focus/PPI, LCC, white balance, and exposure are all set during **[[General Preflight]]**.
- Lights and profiles: DT Stellar/Photon LEDs with the **LED DT Photon** CH profile ([[Mode, Profile & Curve]]).

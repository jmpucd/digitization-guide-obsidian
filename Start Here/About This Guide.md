# About This Guide

This is the digitization lab's working guide — close to an SOP, but kept as a linked **wiki** so it's easy to edit in Obsidian and publish online.

## How it's organized

- **[[Reference]]** — the standards and concepts (what to do and why).
- **[[Preflight]]** — [[General Preflight]], the shared setup every project starts from.
- **[[Imaging Workflow]]** — material-specific procedures that build on preflight. We keep one *worked example* per type (Bodega Bay for bound volumes, Shrem for flat art); the steps generalize.
- **[[Pipeline]]** — how a finished project moves from capture to archive.

## How to use it

For the whole journey on one page, read the **[[Full Workflow (Start to Finish)]]**. Otherwise:

1. Identify your **material type** in [[Imaging Standards by Material Type]].
2. Run **[[General Preflight]]**.
3. Follow the matching **[[Imaging Workflow|workflow]]**.
4. Hand off through the **[[Processing Pipeline Overview|pipeline]]**.

## Conventions

- **Wiki links** `[[like this]]` connect related pages — follow them.
- **Callouts** flag tips, warnings, and sources.
- **📷 Placeholder** callouts mark where a lab screenshot/photo should go. Replace them with real images over time; they describe exactly what to shoot. Images live in `attachments/`.

## Sources

This guide combines our own lab notes with two external references (kept outside the published vault):
- *The Digitization Lab Management Guide v.1* — April Martin, 2025 (material standards, color, naming, handling, equipment). Her figures are **not** reused.
- *DT Digitization Guide — Preflight (Ch. 7)*, 2025 (the Capture One CH preflight steps).

# Handling Archival Materials

Originals come first. These rules apply to **every** project before any imaging starts.

> [!warning] Always
> - No food or drink beyond the desk by the door.
> - **Clean, dry hands.** Wash and fully dry before handling.
> - Use **pencils only** near materials.

---

## Core rules

1. Wear **nitrile gloves** when handling photographs, film, and metal objects.
2. Leave items **on the table** — don't hold them in your hands.
3. Keep items in their **original order**.
4. Don't lean on items; don't place weights directly on ink or painted surfaces.
5. Avoid touching fragile surfaces such as flaking ink.
6. Consult your supervisor before unfolding fragile items.
7. **Don't stress book bindings** — use cradles or wedges where appropriate.
8. Turn pages by lifting from undamaged areas.
9. Store books standing straight up with bookends.
